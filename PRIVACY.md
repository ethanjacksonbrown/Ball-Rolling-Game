# Privacy Policy for Biome Racing

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Biome Racing is a ball-racing game for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what information the app and its advertising partner collect.

## Information the app stores on your device

Coins, unlocked ball skins, equipped power-ups, and sound settings are saved only on your device. The app does not ask you to create an account, and it does not collect your name, email address, phone number, photos, or contacts.

## Advertising

Biome Racing shows ads through Google AdMob. After every second finished race, a full-screen ad may appear. On the results screen you can choose to watch a rewarded ad for extra coins. You can close a full-screen ad, and the rewarded ad is optional.

AdMob may collect:

- Coarse location, such as an approximate location derived from your IP address
- Device ID, including the advertising identifier
- Product interaction, such as how you use the app
- Advertising data, such as which ads you see
- Crash data
- Performance data

If you allow tracking when Apple asks, AdMob may use the device advertising identifier to show more relevant ads and to measure ads. If you decline, the app still works and ads may be less personalized.

You can change this later in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security, then Tracking.

## How this information is used

Ad data is used to show ads, measure how those ads perform, and understand app crashes and performance. Biome Racing does not sell your personal information.

## Children

Biome Racing is rated 4+. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
